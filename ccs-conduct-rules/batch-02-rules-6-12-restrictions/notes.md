## Verified rule anchors (exact)
- **R6** — join/continue membership of association whose objects or activities prejudicial
  to sovereignty & integrity of India, public order or morality → prohibited.
- **R7(i)** — no engaging/participating in demonstration prejudicial to sovereignty &
  integrity, security of State, friendly relations with foreign States, public order,
  decency, morality; or involving contempt of court, defamation, incitement to offence.
  **R7(ii)** — no resorting to/abetting any form of strike, coercion or physical duress in
  connection with any matter pertaining to own service or another GS's service.
- **R8(1)** — previous sanction to own wholly/partly, conduct, or participate in editing or
  management of any newspaper/periodical/electronic media. **(2)** exception: bonafide
  official duty — publishing a book / participating in public media. **(3)** at all times
  make clear views expressed are own, not Government's.
- **R9** — no statement of fact or opinion (radio broadcast, telecast/electronic media,
  document in own name or anonymously/pseudonymously/another's name, communication to
  press, public utterance) that (i) adversely criticises current/recent policy or action of
  Centre or State [proviso: Rule 1(3) 2nd-proviso category — trade-union office-bearer
  bonafide views on service conditions OK]; (ii) embarrasses Centre–State relations;
  (iii) embarrasses relations with a foreign State. Final proviso: official-capacity /
  due-performance statements exempt.
- **R10(1)** — previous sanction to give evidence in any enquiry by any person, committee
  or authority; **(2)** sanctioned evidence: no criticism of Centre/State policy or action;
  **(3)** exceptions: (a) enquiry before authority appointed by Government/Parliament/State
  Legislature; (b) judicial enquiry; (c) departmental enquiry by authorities subordinate
  to Government.
- **R11** — no direct/indirect communication of official document/part/information to any
  GS or person not authorised, except per general/special Government order or good-faith
  performance of duties. **Explanation** — quoting (in representation to HoO/HoD/President)
  from a letter/circular/OM/file-notes one is not authorised to access or keep = unauthorised
  communication.
- **R12** — no asking for/accepting contributions to, or associating with raising of, any
  funds/collections in cash or kind for any object whatsoever without previous sanction of
  Government or prescribed authority.
- **R1(3) 2nd proviso** — Rules 4, 6, 7, 12, 14, 15(3), 16, 18(1)–(3), 19, 20, 21 NOT
  applicable to GS drawing pay not exceeding ₹500 per mensem in non-gazetted posts in
  specified establishments (ports/docks; defence installations except training; PWD
  work-charged; irrigation & electric power; mines; factories; CTO field units).
  **Explanation** — 'establishment' excludes railway establishments and
  admin/managerial/supervisory/security/welfare offices. *(₹500 threshold as written in the
  1964 text — historical; comic must present it as the text's threshold, not as current
  practice.)*
