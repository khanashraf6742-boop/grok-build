## Verified rule anchors (exact)
- **R19(1)** — no GS shall, except with previous sanction of the Government, have recourse
  to any Court or the Press for vindication of any official act subject to adverse criticism
  or a defamatory attack. Proviso — if no sanction received **within three months** from the
  date of receipt of his request by the Government, he is free to assume permission granted.
- **R19(2)** — vindication of private character or private-capacity acts not prohibited;
  where such action is taken → submit a **report to the prescribed authority**.
- **R20** — no GS shall bring or attempt to bring any **political or other outside
  influence** to bear upon any superior authority to further his interests in respect of
  matters pertaining to his service under Government.
- **R21(1)** — no GS shall marry a person having a spouse living. **(2)** — no GS having a
  spouse living shall marry any person. Proviso — Central Government may permit such a
  marriage if satisfied (a) it is permissible under the personal law applicable to the GS
  and the other party, and (b) there are other grounds. **(3)** — GS who has married /
  marries a person other than of Indian nationality → **forthwith intimate** to Government.
- **R22** — GS shall: (a) strictly abide by any law relating to intoxicating drinks or drugs
  in force in any area he may be; (b) not be under the influence during duty + due care
  that performance is not affected at any time; (bb) refrain from **consuming** any
  intoxicating drink or drug in a **public place**; (c) not appear in a public place in a
  state of intoxication; (d) not use any intoxicating drink or drug **to excess**.
  Explanation — 'public place' = any place or premises (including a conveyance) to which
  the public have, or are permitted to have, access, whether on payment or otherwise.
- **R22-A** — no GS shall employ to work any **child below the age of 14 years**.
- **R23** — any question relating to the interpretation of these rules → referred to the
  Government, whose decision shall be final. (GOI decision under the rule: Government of
  India is fully competent to modify, relax or dispense with any rule to prevent hardship
  in an individual case — 1954/1955 OMs.)
- **R24** — Government may, by general or special order, direct that any power exercisable
  by it or any Head of Department under these rules (**except the powers under Rule 23 and
  this rule**) be exercisable also by such officer or authority as may be specified,
  subject to specified conditions.
- **R25** — corresponding rules in force before commencement are repealed; proviso 1 —
  orders made / actions taken under repealed rules deemed made under corresponding
  provisions of these rules; proviso 2 — repeal does not affect the previous operation of
  the repealed rules, and contravention of any of them is punishable as if a contravention
  of these rules.
- **Finale numbers (all from verified texts)**: ₹25,000/₹15,000/₹7,500 report ceilings
  (13(2)); ₹5,000/₹2,000 sanction limits (13(3)); two months' basic pay + one month
  (18(3)); three months deemed permission (19 proviso); one month report (15(2)
  proviso); below 14 years (22-A); at-once commencement (1(2)).
