# BATCH C-1 — RULES 1–5 (incl. 3A, 3B, 3C)

Position as on 25 September 2026. 10 comics. Same visual identity as the CCA series
(4 panels + yellow rule card + green hook banner).

**Validation base:** DoPT consolidated text (as amended up to 31.12.2014, on DoPT website 02.01.2025)
+ verified amendments: Rule 3C substituted 19.11.2014; Rule 3(1)(iv)–(xxi) inserted 27.11.2014
(F.No. 11013/6/2014-Estt.A); Rule 13 (29.07.2019, later batch). Rule-by-rule text cross-checked on
the referencer mirror. [CURRENT]. LAST VERIFIED: 25.09.2026.

- **C1.1 Rule 1** — name; **come into force at once** (contrast: CCA Rules commenced 1.12.1965);
  apply to every person appointed to a civil service or post (including a civilian in Defence
  Service) in connection with the affairs of the Union; NOT applicable to railway servants, AIS
  members, President-excluded posts. ₹500 second proviso in notes only.
  Hook: "At once — across the Union".
- **C1.2 Rule 2** — "the Government" = Central Government; "Government servant" (incl. Defence
  civilians; deemed GS when services placed at a company/corporation/local authority even if salary
  is not from the Consolidated Fund of India); "members of family" (spouse unless judicially
  separated; wholly dependent children/step-children; wholly dependent blood/marriage relatives).
  Hook: "Family = dependent, not just relation".
- **C1.3 Rule 3(1)(i)–(iii)** — absolute integrity; devotion to duty; nothing unbecoming.
  Hook: "Integrity • Devotion • Decorum".
- **C1.4 Rule 3(1)(iv)–(xxi)** — change-detection comic: 18 new duties inserted 27.11.2014
  (Constitution's supremacy; sovereignty & integrity; ethical standards; political neutrality;
  merit & impartiality; accountability & transparency; responsiveness to the weaker sections;
  courtesy; public interest & economical use of resources; declare private interests/conflicts;
  no influencing obligations; no misuse of position; merit alone; non-discrimination; no unlawful
  acts; discipline & lawful orders; confidentiality; professionalism). Hook: "18 new duties — 2014".
- **C1.5 Rule 3(2) + Explanations** — supervisory duty; best judgement unless directed; directions
  ordinarily in writing; oral unavoidable → confirm in writing immediately (both sides' duty);
  habitual failure = lacking devotion (Expl. I); no evasion by seeking instructions (Expl. II).
  Hook: "Oral? Confirm in writing".
- **C1.6 Rule 3A** — no discourteous manner; no dilatory tactics or wilful delay. Hook: "Courteous
  and quick — both".
- **C1.7 Rule 3B** — Government's policies: age of marriage; environment; wildlife; cultural
  heritage; prevention of crime against women. Hook: "National policies, personal duty".
- **C1.8 Rule 3C** [substituted 19.11.2014] — no GS shall indulge in any act of sexual harassment
  of any woman at any work place; incharge's preventive duty; definition (physical contact &
  advances; demand/request for sexual favours; sexually coloured remarks; showing pornography; any
  other unwelcome physical/verbal/non-verbal conduct of sexual nature). Hook: "3C — zero tolerance".
- **C1.9 Rule 4** — 4(1) no influence for family employment; 4(2)(i) Group A: son/daughter/
  dependant in firm with official dealings → previous sanction of the Government (+ urgency
  proviso: provisional + report); 4(2)(ii) intimation duty (+ Group A proviso); 4(3) recusal —
  refer to official superior. Hook: "Sanction BEFORE the job".
- **C1.10 Rule 5** — 5(1) no political party membership/association/assistance; Explanation:
  electoral symbol display = influence; 5(2) family + subversive movements → prevent, else report;
  5(3) Government's decision final; 5(4) no canvassing/interference/influence in elections;
  provisos — right to vote without indicating choice; election duty under law is no contravention.
  Hook: "Vote yes — canvas no".
