Sources: referencer.in mirror (fetched 25.09.2026, consolidated up to 31.12.2014) +

## Verified rule anchors (exact, current)
- **R13(1)** — no GS shall accept, or permit any member of family or any other person acting
  on his behalf to accept, any gift (save as provided in these rules). **Explanation** —
  "gift" includes free transport, boarding, lodging or other service or any other pecuniary
  advantage when provided by any person other than a near relative or personal friend having
  no official dealings with the GS. **Note 1** — casual meal, lift or other social
  hospitality ≠ gift. **Note 2** — avoid accepting lavish or frequent hospitality from any
  individual/industrial or commercial firm/organisation having official dealings.
- **R13(2)** — occasions: weddings, anniversaries, funerals, religious functions, where gift
  conforms to prevailing religious and social practice: may accept from **near relatives or
  personal friends having no official dealings**; **report** to Government if value exceeds
  **₹25,000 Group A / ₹15,000 Group B / ₹7,500 Group C** [substituted 04.03.2014,
  F.No. 11013/3/2013-Estt.(A)].
- **R13(3)** — any other case: no gift **without sanction** if value exceeds **₹5,000
  Group A or B / ₹2,000 Group C** [substituted by G.S.R. 531(E) dated 29.07.2019; earlier
  ₹1,500 A/B and ₹500 C/D].
- **R13(4)** — notwithstanding (2) and (3): GS being member of Indian delegation or
  otherwise may receive and retain gifts from **foreign dignitaries** in accordance with the
  **Foreign Contribution (Acceptance or Retention of Gifts or Presentations) Rules, 2012**,
  as amended [2019 substitution; pre-2019: ≤₹1,000 market value on one occasion].
- **R13(5)** — no gift from any **foreign firm** contracting with GoI or one with which the
  GS had/has/is likely to have official dealings; gifts from any other firm → subject to (3).
- **R13-A Dowry** — no GS shall (i) give or take or abet the giving or taking of dowry;
  (ii) demand directly or indirectly, from the parent or guardian of a bride or bridegroom,
  any dowry. Explanation — 'dowry' has the same meaning as in the **Dowry Prohibition Act,
  1961 (28 of 1961)**.
- **R14** — public demonstrations in honour: no GS shall, except with **previous sanction**,
  receive any complimentary or valedictory address, accept any testimonial, or attend any
  meeting or entertainment held in his honour or in honour of any other GS. Proviso
  exceptions: (i) farewell entertainment of a **substantially private and informal
  character** on retirement/transfer/recently quitted service; (ii) **simple and
  inexpensive** entertainments arranged by public bodies or institutions. **NOTE** — no
  pressure/influence to subscribe towards any farewell entertainment; collection of
  subscriptions from **Class III or Class IV employees** under any circumstances for
  entertainment of any GS not belonging to Class III/IV is forbidden (text's terms).
- **R15(1)** — subject to (2), no GS except with previous sanction shall:
  (a) engage directly/indirectly in any trade or business; (b) negotiate for or undertake
  any other employment; (c) hold an elective office or canvass for candidates for elective
  office in any body, incorporated or not; (d) canvass in support of any business of
  insurance agency, commission agency etc. owned or managed by any member of his family;
  (e) take part (except in official duties) in the registration, promotion or management of
  any bank or company (Companies Act 1956/other law) or co-operative society for commercial
  purposes; (f) participate in/associate with making of (i) sponsored media (radio/TV)
  programme, (ii) Government-media-commissioned but privately produced programme,
  (iii) privately produced media programme incl. video magazine. Proviso — no previous
  permission needed where GS participates in a programme produced or commissioned by
  Government media **in his official capacity**.
- **R15(2)** — WITHOUT previous sanction may: (a) honorary work of a social or charitable
  nature; (b) occasional work of a literary, artistic or scientific character; (c) sports
  activities as an amateur; (d) registration/promotion/management (not elective office) of
  a literary, scientific or charitable society or a club/similar organisation for
  sports/cultural/recreational activities (Societies Registration Act 1860); (e) same for a
  co-operative society substantially for the benefit of GSs (Co-operative Societies Act
  1912). Proviso — (i) discontinue if so directed by Government; (ii) for (d)/(e): official
  duties shall not suffer + **within one month** report to Government with details.
- **R15(3)** — every GS shall **report** if any family member is engaged in a trade or
  business or owns/manages an insurance agency or commission agency.
- **R15(4)** — unless otherwise provided by general/special orders, no fee for any work done
  for any private or public body or any private person **without sanction of the prescribed
  authority**. Explanation — 'fee' as in **FR 9(6-A)**.
- **R15-A** — sub-letting and vacation of Government accommodation: (1) no GS shall sub-let,
  lease or otherwise allow occupation by any other person of Government accommodation
  allotted to him; (2) after cancellation of allotment, vacate within the time-limit
  prescribed by the allotting authority.
