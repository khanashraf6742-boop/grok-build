## RULE 1 — Short title, commencement and application
- ✅ 1(1) — name: Central Civil Services (Conduct) Rules, 1964 [COMIC C1.1]
- ✅ 1(2) — **come into force at once** [COMIC C1.1]
- ✅ 1(3) — apply to every person appointed to a civil service or post **(including a civilian in Defence Service)** in connection with the affairs of the Union; subject to IFS (Conduct and Discipline) Rules, 1961 [COMIC C1.1]
- ✅ 1(3) 1st proviso — NOT applicable: (a) railway servants (s.3, Indian Railways Act 1890; Railway Board posts; posts under Railway Board/Financial Commissioner); (b) AIS members; (c) President-excluded posts [COMIC C1.1]
- ✅ 1(3) 2nd proviso — Rules 4, 6, 7, 12, 14, 15(3), 16, 18(1)–(3), 19, 20, 21 do not apply to GS drawing pay **not exceeding ₹500 per mensem** in non-gazetted posts in specified establishments (ports/docks; defence installations except training; PWD work-charged; irrigation & electric power; mines; factories; CTO field units) [COMIC C2.8 — threshold presented as the 1964 text's, historical]
- ✅ 1(3) 3rd proviso — temporary transfer to a clause-(a) service: rules still apply [notes]
- ✅ Explanation to 2nd proviso — 'establishment' excludes railway establishments and admin/managerial/supervisory/security/welfare offices [COMIC C2.8]

## RULE 2 — Definitions
- ✅ 2(a) — "the Government" = the Central Government [COMIC C1.2]
- ✅ 2(b) — "Government servant": any person appointed by Government to any civil service or post in connection with the affairs of the Union, **includes a civilian in a Defence Service** + Explanation (services placed at disposal of a company/corporation/organisation/local authority → deemed GS even if salary is not drawn from the Consolidated Fund of India) [COMIC C1.2]
- ✅ 2(c) — "members of family": (i) wife/husband (unless separated by decree/order of a competent Court); (ii) son, daughter, step-son, step-daughter **wholly dependent** (excluding independent children / custody removed by law); (iii) any other person related by blood or marriage **wholly dependent** [COMIC C1.2]

## RULE 3 — General
- ✅ 3(1)(i)–(iii) — absolute integrity; devotion to duty; nothing unbecoming of a Government servant [COMIC C1.3]
- ✅ 3(1)(iv)–(xxi) — **18 clauses inserted 27.11.2014**: supremacy of the Constitution & democratic values; sovereignty & integrity of India, security of State, public order, decency, morality; high ethical standards & honesty; **political neutrality**; merit, fairness, impartiality; **accountability & transparency**; responsiveness to the public, particularly the weaker sections; courtesy & good behaviour with the public; public interest & economical use of public resources; **declare private interests & resolve conflicts**; no financial/other obligations that may influence; **no misuse of position** for benefit of self/family/friends; merit alone in choices & recommendations; fairness & non-discrimination (especially the poor & under-privileged); refrain from anything contrary to law/rules/established practices; discipline & implementation of lawful orders; **confidentiality**; highest professionalism & dedication [COMIC C1.4]
- ✅ 3(2)(i) — supervisory post → ensure integrity & devotion to duty of all under control [COMIC C1.5]
- ✅ 3(2)(ii) — act in best judgement except under direction of official superior [COMIC C1.5]
- ✅ 3(2)(iii) — directions ordinarily in writing; oral unavoidable → superior confirms in writing immediately thereafter [COMIC C1.5]
- ✅ 3(2)(iv) — receiver of oral direction seeks written confirmation; duty of superior to confirm [COMIC C1.5]
- ✅ Explanation I — habitual failure to perform tasks within time/expected quality = lacking devotion to duty [COMIC C1.5 + notes]
- ✅ Explanation II — clause (ii) no licence to evade responsibility by seeking instructions [COMIC C1.5 + notes]

## RULE 3-A — Promptness and Courtesy
- ✅ 3A(a) — no discourteous manner in performance of official duties [COMIC C1.6]
- ✅ 3A(b) — no dilatory tactics or wilful delay in disposal of work, in official dealings with the public or otherwise [COMIC C1.6]

## RULE 3-B — Observance of Government's policies
- ✅ 3B(i) — act in accordance with policies on age of marriage, preservation of environment, protection of wildlife and cultural heritage [COMIC C1.7]
- ✅ 3B(ii) — observe policies regarding prevention of crime against women [COMIC C1.7]

## RULE 3-C — Prohibition of sexual harassment of working women [substituted 19.11.2014]
- ✅ 3C(1) — no GS shall indulge in any act of sexual harassment of any woman at any work place [COMIC C1.8]
- ✅ 3C(2) — every GS in charge of a work place shall take appropriate steps to prevent sexual harassment [COMIC C1.8]
- ✅ 3C Explanation (I)(a) — "sexual harassment" includes: physical contact & advances; demand/request for sexual favours; sexually coloured remarks; showing pornography; any other unwelcome physical/verbal/non-verbal conduct of sexual nature [COMIC C1.8 + notes]
- ⚠️ 3C Explanation (I)(b) — circumstances amounting to SH (preferential treatment promise; detrimental threat; employment-status threat; interference/hostile environment; humiliating treatment) [notes]
- ⚠️ 3C Explanation (I)(c) — "workplace" definition (Government-funded establishments; hospitals/nursing homes; sports institutes; any place visited in the course of employment incl. transportation; dwelling place/house) [notes]

## RULE 4 — Employment of near relatives in companies or firms
- ✅ 4(1) — no GS shall use position or influence directly/indirectly to secure employment for any family member in any company or firm [COMIC C1.9]
- ✅ 4(2)(i) — Group 'A' officer: son/daughter/dependant accepting employment in a company/firm with which he has official dealings, or any other company/firm having official dealings with the Government → **previous sanction of the Government** + proviso (urgent → provisional acceptance subject to permission, matter reported) [COMIC C1.9]
- ✅ 4(2)(ii) — intimation duty on becoming aware of family member's employment + whether he has/had official dealings + proviso (Group A exempt from intimation if already sanctioned/reported) [COMIC C1.9]
- ✅ 4(3) — recusal: no dealing with matter/contract where family member is employed or family is interested → **refer to official superior** [COMIC C1.9]

## RULE 5 — Taking part in politics and elections
- ✅ 5(1) — no membership of/association with any political party or organisation taking part in politics; no taking part in, subscribing in aid of, or assisting any political movement or activity [COMIC C1.10]
- ✅ 5(1) Explanation — display of any electoral symbol on person, vehicle or residence = using influence in connection with an election [COMIC C1.10]
- ✅ 5(2) — duty to endeavour to prevent family members from subversive movements/activities; if unable → **report to the Government** [COMIC C1.10]
- ✅ 5(3) — whether a party is political / organisation takes part in politics / movement subversive → **Government's decision final** [COMIC C1.10]
- ✅ 5(4) — no canvassing, interference, influence or participation in elections to any legislature or local authority + provisos ((i) right to vote — with no indication of voting intent; (ii) election duty under law no contravention) [COMIC C1.10]

## RULE 6 — Joining of associations by Government servants
- ✅ No joining or continuing membership of an association whose **objects or activities** are prejudicial to the **sovereignty and integrity of India, public order or morality** [COMIC C2.1]

## RULE 7 — Demonstration and Strikes
- ✅ 7(i) — no engaging/participating in any demonstration prejudicial to sovereignty & integrity, security of the State, friendly relations with foreign States, public order, decency, morality; or involving contempt of court, defamation or incitement to an offence [COMIC C2.2]
- ✅ 7(ii) — no resorting to or in any way abetting any form of strike, coercion or physical duress in connection with any matter pertaining to own service or another GS's service [COMIC C2.2]

## RULE 8 — Connection with Press or other media
- ✅ 8(1) — no owning wholly/in part, conducting, or participating in editing/management of any newspaper, periodical or **electronic media** without **previous sanction of the Government** [COMIC C2.3]
- ✅ 8(2) — exception: GS in **bonafide discharge of official duties** publishes a book / participates in a public media [COMIC C2.3]
- ✅ 8(3) — must **at all times** make clear the views expressed are his own, not Government's [COMIC C2.3]
## RULE 9 — Criticism of Government
- ✅ 9 chapeau — covers statements of fact **or opinion** via: radio broadcast, telecast/electronic media, documents in own name/Anonymously/pseudonymously/in another's name, communications to the press, public utterances [COMIC C2.4]
- ✅ 9(i) — no adverse criticism of any **current or recent policy or action** of the Central or a State Government; proviso — Rule 1(3) 2nd-proviso category: bonafide trade-union/association office-bearer views safeguarding/improving conditions of service OK [COMIC C2.4]
- ✅ 9(ii)+(iii) — nothing capable of embarrassing relations between the Centre and any State Government, or the Centre and any foreign State [COMIC C2.4]
- ✅ 9 final proviso — statements/views in **official capacity or due performance of duties** exempt [COMIC C2.4]
## RULE 10 — Evidence before Committee or any other authority
- ✅ 10(1) — no evidence in connection with any enquiry by any person, committee or authority except with **previous sanction of the Government** [COMIC C2.5]
- ✅ 10(2) — sanctioned evidence: **no criticism** of the policy or action of the Centre or a State Government [COMIC C2.5]
- ✅ 10(3)(a)–(c) — exceptions (no sanction needed): enquiries before authorities appointed by Government/Parliament/State Legislature; **judicial enquiries**; departmental enquiries ordered by authorities subordinate to the Government [COMIC C2.5]
## RULE 11 — Communication of official information
- ✅ 11 — no direct or indirect communication of any official document/part/information to any GS or person **not authorised**, except per general/special Government order or good-faith performance of duties [COMIC C2.6]
- ✅ 11 Explanation — quoting (in a representation to HoO/HoD/President) from a letter/circular/OM/file-notes one is not authorised to access or keep = **unauthorised communication** [COMIC C2.6]
## RULE 12 — Subscriptions
- ✅ 12 — no asking for or accepting contributions to, or associating with the raising of, any funds or collections **in cash or in kind, for any object whatsoever**, without previous sanction of the Government or the **prescribed authority** [COMIC C2.7]
## RULE 13 — Gifts
- ✅ 13(1) — no GS shall accept, or permit any member of family or any other person acting on his behalf to accept, any gift (save as provided in these rules) [COMIC C3.1]
- ✅ 13(1) Explanation — "gift" includes free transport, boarding, lodging or other service or any other pecuniary advantage when provided by any person other than a near relative or personal friend having no official dealings [COMIC C3.1]
- ✅ 13(1) Note 1 — casual meal, lift or other social hospitality ≠ gift; Note 2 — avoid lavish/frequent hospitality from those with official dealings [COMIC C3.1]
- ✅ 13(2) — weddings, anniversaries, funerals, religious functions: gifts from near relatives / personal friends having no official dealings; **report** if value exceeds **₹25,000 Gr. A / ₹15,000 Gr. B / ₹7,500 Gr. C** [substituted 04.03.2014] [COMIC C3.2]
- ✅ 13(3) — any other case: no gift without **sanction** if value exceeds **₹5,000 Gr. A/B / ₹2,000 Gr. C** [G.S.R. 531(E), 29.07.2019; pre-2019 ₹1,500/₹500] [COMIC C3.3]
- ✅ 13(4) — gifts from foreign dignitaries: receive/retain per **Foreign Contribution (Acceptance or Retention of Gifts or Presentations) Rules, 2012**, as amended [2019 substitution; pre-2019 ₹1,000-on-one-occasion rule] [COMIC C3.4]
- ✅ 13(5) — no gifts from any foreign firm contracting with GoI or having/likely official dealings; other foreign firms → 13(3) limits [COMIC C3.4]

## RULE 13-A — Dowry
- ✅ 13-A(i) — no giving or taking or abetting the giving or taking of dowry [COMIC C3.5]
- ✅ 13-A(ii) — no demanding directly or indirectly from the parent or guardian of a bride or bridegroom; Explanation — 'dowry' per the Dowry Prohibition Act, 1961 (28 of 1961) [COMIC C3.5]
## RULE 14 — Public demonstrations in honour of Government servants
- ✅ 14 — no receiving any complimentary or valedictory address, accepting any testimonial, or attending any meeting/entertainment held in his honour or another GS's honour, except with **previous sanction** [COMIC C3.6]
- ✅ 14 proviso (i) — farewell entertainment of a substantially private and informal character (retirement/transfer/recently quitted) exempt [COMIC C3.6]
- ✅ 14 proviso (ii) — simple and inexpensive entertainments by public bodies or institutions exempt [COMIC C3.6]
- ✅ 14 NOTE — no pressure/influence to subscribe towards farewell entertainment; no collection of subscriptions from Class III/Class IV employees under any circumstances for entertainment of a GS not belonging to Class III/IV (text's terms) [COMIC C3.6]
## RULE 15 — Private trade or employment
- ✅ 15(1)(a)–(f) — previous sanction needed to: engage in trade/business; negotiate/undertake other employment; hold/canvass for elective office in any body; canvass for family-owned insurance/commission agency; take part in registration/promotion/management of bank/company/commercial co-op; participate in sponsored or privately produced media programmes [COMIC C3.7]
- ✅ 15(1) proviso — no permission needed for Government-media programmes in official capacity [COMIC C3.7]
- ✅ 15(2)(a)–(e) — without sanction: honorary social/charitable work; occasional literary/artistic/scientific work; amateur sports; literary/scientific/charitable societies & sports/cultural clubs (Soc. Registration Act 1860); staff co-operative societies (Co-op Societies Act 1912) [COMIC C3.8]
- ✅ 15(2) proviso — discontinue if directed; for (d)/(e) duties must not suffer + **report within one month** with details [COMIC C3.8]
- ✅ 15(3) — report if any family member is engaged in trade/business or owns/manages an insurance agency or commission agency [COMIC C3.9]
- ✅ 15(4) — no fee for work for any private/public body or person without sanction of the prescribed authority; Explanation — 'fee' per FR 9(6-A) [COMIC C3.9]

## RULE 15-A — Sub-letting and vacation of Government accommodation
- ✅ 15-A(1) — no sub-letting, leasing or otherwise allowing occupation by any other person of allotted Government accommodation [COMIC C3.10]
- ✅ 15-A(2) — after cancellation of allotment, vacate within the time-limit prescribed by the allotting authority [COMIC C3.10]
## RULE 16 — Investment, lending and borrowing
- ✅ 16(1) — no speculation in any stock, share or other investment; proviso — occasional investments through stock brokers or duly authorised/licensed/registered persons OK; Explanation — frequent purchase or sale (or both) = deemed speculation [COMIC C4.1]
- ✅ 16(2) — no investment (self / family / person acting on his behalf) likely to embarrass or influence him in discharge of official duties; purchase of shares out of quotas reserved for Directors of Companies or their friends and associates = deemed such an investment [COMIC C4.2]
- ✅ 16(3) — whether a transaction is of the 16(1)/16(2) nature → Government's decision final [COMIC C4.2]
- ✅ 16(4)(i) — save in the ordinary course of business with a bank or a public limited company: no lending/borrowing/deposits with any person, firm or private limited company within local limits of authority or likely official dealings; no pecuniary obligations; no lending at interest (money or kind). Provisos: small temporary interest-free loan with relative/personal friend; credit account with bona fide tradesman; advance of pay to private employee; anything with Government's previous sanction [COMIC C4.3]
- ✅ 16(4)(ii) — appointed/transferred to a post involving breach of 16(2)/16(4) → forthwith report to the prescribed authority and act per its order [COMIC C4.3]
## RULE 17 — Insolvency and habitual indebtedness
- ✅ 17 — manage private affairs to avoid habitual indebtedness or insolvency; legal proceeding for recovery of any debt or for adjudging insolvent → forthwith report the full facts to the Government [COMIC C4.4]
- ✅ 17 NOTE — burden of proving the insolvency/indebtedness was not foreseeable with ordinary diligence / beyond control, and not from extravagant or dissipated habits, is on the Government servant [COMIC C4.4]
## RULE 18 — Movable, immovable and valuable property
- ✅ 18(1)(i) — first appointment: return of assets and liabilities — (a) immovable (inherited/owned/acquired/lease/mortgage; own, family or other person's name); (b) shares, debentures, cash incl. bank deposits; (c) other movable property; (d) debts and liabilities [COMIC C4.5]
- ✅ 18(1)(i) Notes — Group 'D' ordinarily exempt (Government may direct); movable items under ₹10,000 may be lump-summed, daily-use articles excluded; no fresh return on appointment to another service/post [COMIC C4.5]
- ✅ 18(1)(ii) — Group 'A' & 'B': annual return of immovable property particulars [COMIC C4.5]
- ✅ 18(2) — immovable property acquire/dispose (lease, mortgage, purchase, sale, gift or otherwise; own or family name) only with previous knowledge of the prescribed authority; proviso — previous sanction if with a person having official dealings [COMIC C4.6]
- ✅ 18(3) — movable property transaction (own/family name): report within one month if value exceeds two months' basic pay; proviso — previous sanction if with a person having official dealings [G.S.R. 370(E), 09.05.2011] [COMIC C4.7]
- ✅ 18(4) — Government/prescribed authority may at any time require a full and complete statement of movable/immovable property (self/family), within a specified period, including the means/source of acquisition [COMIC C4.8]
- ✅ 18(5) — Group 'C'/'D' exemptions possible for any provision except sub-rule (4); no exemption without concurrence of the Cabinet Secretariat (Department of Personnel) [COMIC C4.8]
- ✅ 18 Expl. I(1) — movable property includes jewellery; insurance policies (annual premium exceeding two months' basic pay — 2011 substitution); shares, securities, debentures; all loans secured or not; motor cars, motor cycles, horses, other conveyance; refrigerators, radios, radiograms, TVs [COMIC C4.9]
- ✅ 18 Expl. I(2) — prescribed authority: Government (Gr. A, unless lower specified); Head of Department (Gr. B); Head of Office (Gr. C/D); deputation → parent cadre department [COMIC C4.9]
- ✅ 18 Expl. II — 'lease' (except with person having official dealings) = year-to-year, term exceeding one year, or reserving a yearly rent [COMIC C4.9]

## RULE 18-A — Restrictions: immovable property outside India & transactions with foreigners
- ✅ 18-A(a) — previous sanction to acquire immovable property situated outside India (own/family name) [COMIC C4.10]
- ✅ 18-A(b) — previous sanction to dispose of / grant lease in respect of immovable property outside India [COMIC C4.10]
- ✅ 18-A(c) — previous sanction for any transaction with any foreigner, foreign Government, foreign organisation or concern for acquisition/disposal of immovable property; Explanation — prescribed authority as in Rule 18 [COMIC C4.10]
## RULE 19 — Vindication of acts and character of Government servant
- ✅ 19(1) — no recourse to any Court or the Press for vindication of any official act (adverse criticism / defamatory attack) except with previous sanction of the Government; proviso — no sanction within **three months** of receipt of the request by Government → free to assume permission granted [COMIC C5.1]
- ✅ 19(2) — vindication of private character / private-capacity acts not prohibited; such action → report to the prescribed authority [COMIC C5.1]
## RULE 20 — Canvassing of non-official or other outside influence
- ✅ 20 — no bringing or attempting to bring any political or other outside influence to bear upon any superior authority to further the GS's interests in respect of matters pertaining to his service under Government [COMIC C5.2]
## RULE 21 — Restriction regarding marriage
- ✅ 21(1) — no GS shall marry a person having a spouse living [COMIC C5.3]
- ✅ 21(2) — no GS having a spouse living shall marry any person [COMIC C5.3]
- ✅ 21 proviso — Central Government may permit such marriage if satisfied: (a) permissible under the personal law applicable to the GS and the other party; (b) other grounds exist [COMIC C5.3]
- ✅ 21(3) — marriage with a person other than of Indian nationality → forthwith intimate the fact to the Government [COMIC C5.3]
## RULE 22 — Consumption of intoxicating drinks and drugs
- ✅ 22(a) — strictly abide by any law relating to intoxicating drinks or drugs in force in any area the GS may be [COMIC C5.4]
- ✅ 22(b) — not under the influence of any intoxicating drink or drug during duty; due care that performance of duties at any time is not affected [COMIC C5.4]
- ✅ 22(bb) — refrain from consuming any intoxicating drink or drug in a public place [COMIC C5.4]
- ✅ 22(c) — not appear in a public place in a state of intoxication; 22(d) — not use any intoxicating drink or drug to excess [COMIC C5.4]
- ✅ 22 Explanation — 'public place' = any place or premises (including a conveyance) to which the public have, or are permitted to have, access, whether on payment or otherwise [COMIC C5.4]

## RULE 22-A — Prohibition regarding employment of children below 14 years of age
- ✅ 22-A — no GS shall employ to work any child below the age of 14 years [COMIC C5.5]

## RULE 23 — Interpretation
- ✅ 23 — any question relating to the interpretation of these rules → referred to the Government, whose decision shall be final [COMIC C5.6]
- ✅ 23 GOI decision — Government of India fully competent to modify, relax or dispense with any rule to prevent hardship in an individual case (MHA 1954/1955 OMs) [COMIC C5.6]

## RULE 24 — Delegation of powers
- ✅ 24 — Government may by general or special order direct that any power exercisable by it or any Head of Department under these rules (**except powers under Rule 23 and Rule 24 itself**) be exercisable also by any specified officer or authority, subject to specified conditions [COMIC C5.7]

## RULE 25 — Repeal and saving
- ✅ 25 — corresponding rules in force before commencement repealed; proviso 1 — orders made / actions taken under repealed rules deemed made under corresponding provisions of these rules [COMIC C5.8]
- ✅ 25 proviso 2 — repeal does not affect previous operation of repealed rules; contravention of any of them punishable as if contravention of these rules [COMIC C5.8]
