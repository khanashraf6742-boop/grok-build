# BATCH C-4 — Rules 16, 17, 18, 18-A (10 comics)

Sources: referencer.in mirror fetched 25.09.2026 (consolidated up to 31.12.2014, incl.
G.S.R. 370(E) 09.05.2011 amendment text shown in-line). Rule 19 → Batch C-5.

## Verified rule anchors (exact)
- **R16(1)** — no speculation in any stock, share or other investment. Proviso: occasional
  investments through stock brokers or other persons duly authorised and licensed or holding
  a certificate of registration under the relevant law are outside the ban. Explanation —
  **frequent purchase or sale** (or both) of shares, securities or other investments is
  deemed speculation.
- **R16(2)** — no investment (self, family, or person acting on his behalf) likely to
  **embarrass or influence** him in discharge of official duties; purchase of shares out of
  quotas reserved for **Directors of Companies or their friends and associates** is deemed
  such an investment.
- **R16(3)** — whether a transaction is of the 16(1)/16(2) nature → **Government's decision
  final**.
- **R16(4)(i)** — save in the **ordinary course of business with a bank or a public limited
  company**: no lending/borrowing/depositing money (as principal or agent), to/from/with any
  person, firm or **private limited company** within the local limits of his authority or
  with whom he is likely to have official dealings, or otherwise placing himself under any
  pecuniary obligation; and no lending money at interest or with return in money or kind.
  Proviso 1 — may give/accept from a **relative or personal friend** a purely temporary loan
  of a small amount **free of interest**, operate a credit account with a **bona fide
  tradesman**, or make an advance of pay to his private employee. Proviso 2 — previous
  sanction of Government exempts any transaction.
- **R16(4)(ii)** — appointed/transferred to a post that would involve breach of 16(2) or
  16(4) → **forthwith report** the circumstances to the prescribed authority and act per
  its order.
- **R17** — manage private affairs to avoid habitual indebtedness or insolvency; legal
  proceeding instituted for recovery of any debt or for adjudging him insolvent →
  **forthwith report the full facts** to the Government. NOTE — burden of proving the
  insolvency/indebtedness resulted from circumstances not foreseeable with ordinary
  diligence or beyond his control, and not from extravagant or dissipated habits, is on the
  Government servant.
- **R18(1)(i)** — on **first appointment**: return of assets and liabilities in prescribed
  form — (a) immovable property inherited/owned/acquired/held on lease or mortgage (own
  name, family member's name, or another person's name); (b) shares, debentures, cash
  including bank deposits; (c) other movable property; (d) debts and other liabilities.
  Note 1 — (1) ordinarily not for Group 'D' (Government may direct). Note 2 — movable items
  worth less than ₹10,000 may be lump-summed; daily-use articles (clothes, utensils,
  crockery, books) need not be included. Note 3 — GS already in service appointed to
  another service/post → no fresh return needed.
- **R18(1)(ii)** — every Group 'A' and Group 'B' GS: **annual return** of immovable property
  particulars (inherited/owned/acquired/held on lease or mortgage, own or family name).
- **R18(2)** — no acquiring or disposing of **immovable** property by lease, mortgage,
  purchase, sale, gift or otherwise (own or family name) except with the **previous
  knowledge** of the prescribed authority; proviso — **previous sanction** if the
  transaction is with a person having official dealings with him.
- **R18(3)** [amended G.S.R. 370(E), 09.05.2011] — transaction in respect of **movable**
  property (own or family name): **within one month** report to the prescribed authority if
  value exceeds **two months' basic pay**; proviso — previous sanction if with a person
  having official dealings.
- **R18(4)** — Government/prescribed authority may at any time, by general or special
  order, require a **full and complete statement** of movable or immovable property held or
  acquired (self/family), within a specified period, including details of the **means by
  which, or the source from which**, the property was acquired.
- **R18(5)** — Government may exempt any category of Group 'C'/'D' GSs from any provision
  of this rule **except sub-rule (4)**; no exemption without the concurrence of the Cabinet
  Secretariat (Department of Personnel).
- **R18 Expl. I(1)** — movable property includes (a) jewellery; insurance policies with
  annual premium exceeding **two months' basic pay** [2011 substitution — earlier ₹10,000
  or one-sixth of annual emoluments, whichever less]; shares, securities, debentures;
  (b) all loans, secured or not; (c) motor cars, motor cycles, horses or other means of
  conveyance; (d) refrigerators, radios, radiograms, television sets.
- **R18 Expl. I(2)** — prescribed authority: Government for Group 'A' (unless lower
  authority specified); Head of Department for Group 'B'; Head of Office for Group 'C'/'D';
  for foreign service/deputation — parent cadre department.
- **R18 Expl. II** — 'lease' (except with a person having official dealings) = lease from
  year to year or for a term exceeding one year or reserving a yearly rent.
- **R18-A** — notwithstanding 18(2), no GS shall except with the **previous sanction** of
  the prescribed authority: (a) acquire immovable property situated **outside India**
  (own/family name); (b) dispose of or grant any lease in respect of such property;
  (c) enter into any transaction with any **foreigner, foreign Government, foreign
  organisation or concern** for acquisition/disposal of immovable property. Explanation —
  prescribed authority as in Rule 18.
