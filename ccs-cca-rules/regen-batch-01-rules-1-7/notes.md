## Verified anchors (exact)
- **R1** — (1) Central Civil Services (Classification, Control & Appeal) Rules, 1965;
  (2) in force **1 December 1965**.
- **R2** — Interpretation: (a) appointing authority — four possible authorities
  (empowered to appoint to the Service/grade; to the post; which appointed him; which
  appointed him to the earlier Service/post in continuous employment) — **whichever is the
  highest**; (b) cadre authority — per the Service's own rules; (c) Central Civil
  Service/post includes civilian Service/post of the corresponding Group in the Defence
  Services; (d) Commission = **UPSC**; (e) Defence Services = services under GoI in MoD,
  paid from Defence Services Estimates, **not subject to** the Army Act 1950 / Navy Act
  1957 / Air Force Act 1950; (f) Department = establishment/organization declared by the
  **President by notification**; (g) disciplinary authority = authority competent to
  impose penalties specified in **Rule 11**; (h) Government servant — three limbs: member
  of Service/civil post under the Union (incl. foreign service or services placed at
  disposal of State/local authority); State GS temporarily placed at Central disposal;
  local/other-authority servant temporarily placed at Central disposal; (i) head of
  department — authority declared under FSR/CSR; (j) head of office — under GFR;
  (k) Schedule = Schedule to these rules; (l) Secretary — incl. Special/Additional
  Secretary, JS in independent charge, Cabinet Secretary (Cabinet Secretariat), Secretary
  to the President (or Military Secretary), Secretary to the PM, Planning Commission
  Secretary/Additional Secretary; (m) Service = civil service of the Union.
- **R3** — (1) applies to every GS incl. every civilian GS in the Defence Services; NOT
  (a) railway servants (R.102, Vol I IR Establishment Code); (b) AIS members; (c) casual
  employment; (d) persons subject to discharge on less than one month's notice; (e) persons
  with special provision by law or President-approved agreement. (2) President may by
  order exclude any Group. (3) temporary transfer to an excepted Service/post — rules
  still apply. (4) doubts → **President decides**.
- **R4** — Classification of Services: Groups A, B, C, D; (2) different grades of a
  Service may fall in different Groups.
- **R5** — Constitution: the four Groups consist of the Services and grades **specified
  in the Schedule**.
- **R6** — Classification of posts: civil posts (other than those ordinarily held by
  persons to whom these rules don't apply) classified by **general or special order of the
  President** into the four Groups. **6-A** — Class I/II/III/IV references in all
  pre-commencement instruments construed as Group A/B/C/D; "Class" → "Group".
- **R7** — General Central Service: posts of any Group not included in any other CCS →
  deemed included in the **General Central Service** of the corresponding Group; appointee
  deemed member unless already a member of another CCS of the same Group.
