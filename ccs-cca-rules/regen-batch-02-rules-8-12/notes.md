# CCA REGEN BATCH R2 — Rules 8–11 (10 comics) — Rule 12 → R3

Verified 25.09.2026 from referencer.in CCA mirror (rule text incl. 2009 & 2014 amendments).

## Verified anchors (exact)
- **R8** — ALL appointments to Central Civil Services/Posts Group 'A' made by the
  **President**; proviso — President may delegate (general/special order, conditions).
- **R9** — (1) appointments to CCS (other than GCS) Groups B/C/D by authorities specified
  in the **Schedule**; proviso — Group C/D civilian services/posts in Defence Services:
  officers empowered by those authorities. (2) GCS posts B/C/D — authorities per
  President's general/special order, else the Schedule authorities.
- **R10(1)** — suspension by: appointing authority / any authority subordinate to it /
  disciplinary authority / President-empowered authority. Grounds: (a) disciplinary
  proceeding contemplated or pending; (aa) engaged in activities prejudicial to security
  of the State; (b) criminal-offence case under investigation, inquiry or trial. Proviso —
  order by authority lower than appointing authority → forthwith report circumstances to
  appointing authority (C&AG exception re IA&AS/Asst AG).
- **R10(2)** — DEEMED suspension by order of appointing authority: (a) from date of
  detention if detained in custody (criminal charge or otherwise) exceeding **48 hours**;
  (b) from date of conviction if sentenced to imprisonment exceeding 48 hours and not
  forthwith dismissed/removed/compulsorily retired. Explanation — 48 hours in (b)
  computed from commencement of imprisonment; intermittent periods counted.
- **R10(3)** — dismissal/removal/compulsory retirement set aside in appeal/review and case
  remitted → suspension deemed continued from date of original penalty order, until
  further orders.
- **R10(4)** — penalty set aside/void by Court decision and further inquiry ordered
  (proviso: only where Court decided purely on technical grounds) → deemed suspension
  from date of original penalty order, until further orders.
- **R10(5)** — (a) suspension continues until modified/revoked by competent authority;
  (b) new disciplinary proceeding during suspension → may direct continuation until
  termination of all/any proceedings, reasons recorded in writing; (c) modification/
  revocation by the authority which made (or is deemed to have made) the order or any
  authority to which it is subordinate.
- **R10(6)** — review before expiry of **90 days** from effective date, on Review
  Committee recommendation; extend or revoke; subsequent reviews before expiry of the
  extended period; extension **not exceeding 180 days at a time**.
- **R10(7)** — suspension not valid after 90 days unless extended after review before
  expiry; proviso — deemed suspension under (2): review not necessary while still in
  custody at 90 days; the 90 days counted from release from detention or intimation of
  release to appointing authority, whichever is later.
- **R11 minor** — (i) censure; (ii) withholding of promotion; (iii) recovery from pay of
  whole/part of pecuniary loss caused to Government by negligence or breach of orders;
  (iiia) reduction to a lower stage in the time-scale by ONE stage, ≤ 3 years, without
  cumulative effect, not adversely affecting pension; (iv) withholding increments.
- **R11 major** — (v) reduction to lower stage in time-scale for specified period with
  directions on increments/postponement [save as in clause (iii)(a)]; (vi) reduction to
  lower time-scale of pay, grade, post or Service for specified period — bar to promotion
  during that period — with directions re postponement of increments and regaining
  original seniority [substituted 2009, F.No. 11012/2/2005-Estt.(A)]; (vii) compulsory
  retirement; (viii) removal — NOT a disqualification for future employment;
  (ix) dismissal — ordinarily a disqualification for future employment.
- **R11 provisos** — (1) charge of possession of assets disproportionate to known sources
  of income OR acceptance of gratification (other than legal remuneration) as motive/
  reward established → removal or dismissal SHALL be imposed; (2) exceptional case +
  special reasons recorded in writing → any other penalty.
- **R11 Explanation (not penalties)** — (i) increments withheld for failing a departmental
  exam; (ii) efficiency-bar stoppage; (iii) non-promotion after consideration; (iv)
  reversion of officiating GS (unsuitability/administrative grounds); (v) probationer
  reversion per terms; (vi) return of borrowed State-government servant; (vii)
  superannuation compulsory retirement; (viii) termination of probationer/temporary
  servant per terms (CCS Temporary Service Rules 1965 R.5(1))/agreement; (ix) compensation
  awarded on Complaints Committee recommendation in sexual-harassment inquiries [inserted
  19.11.2014, F.No. 11013/2/2014-Estt.(A), cross-ref Conduct Rule 3C].
- **R12** (verified, for R3) — disciplinary authorities: President any GS; Schedule/
  appointing/empowered authorities; Gr. C (other than CSCS)/D — Secretary (Ministry) or
  head of office; (4)(a) major penalties (v)–(ix) never by authority subordinate to
  appointing authority (C&AG exception); (b) consultation on temporary appointment;
  (c) LBSNAA Director — censure/recovery after Rule-16 procedure.
