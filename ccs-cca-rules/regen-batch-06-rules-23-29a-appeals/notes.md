- **R23 — Orders against which appeal lies** (subject to Rule 22): (i) an order of suspension
  made or deemed made under Rule 10; (ii) an order imposing any Rule 11 penalty, whether by
  the disciplinary authority or by any appellate or revising authority; (iii) an order
  enhancing any Rule 11 penalty; (iv) an order which denies or varies to his disadvantage
  pay, allowances, pension or other conditions of service, or interprets a rule/agreement
  to his disadvantage; (v) orders — (a) stopping him at the efficiency bar on ground of
  unfitness; (b) reverting him while officiating in a higher service/grade/post otherwise
  than as a penalty; (c) reducing/withholding pension or denying maximum pension;
  (d) determining subsistence and other allowances for the suspension period;
  (e) determining pay and allowances for the suspension period or from dismissal/removal/
  compulsory retirement/reduction until reinstatement or restoration; (f) determining
  whether that period is treated as duty. Explanation: 'Government servant' includes a
  person who has ceased to be in Government service; 'pension' includes additional
  pension, gratuity and other retirement benefits.
- **R24 — Appellate authority**: (1) appeal to the authority specified in the Schedule or
  by a general/special order of the President; where none specified — (i) Group A/B:
  (a) appointing authority where the order-maker is subordinate to it, (b) else the
  President; (ii) Group C/D: the authority to which the order-maker is immediately
  subordinate. (2)(i) Rule 18 common proceedings → authority over the DA for that
  proceeding (President where he is the appellate authority); (ii) if the order-maker later
  becomes the appellate authority → the authority immediately over him. (3) penalty
  imposed for activities connected with work as office-bearer of an association,
  federation or union participating in the Joint Consultation and Compulsory Arbitration
  Scheme → appeal to the President where no such appeal lies otherwise.
- **R25 — Period of limitation**: appeal within 45 days from the date a copy of the order
  is delivered to the appellant; proviso — appellate authority may entertain a late appeal
  if satisfied there was sufficient cause.
- **R26 — Form and contents**: (1) separately and in own name; (2) presented to the
  appellate authority, copy forwarded to the order-maker; all material statements and
  arguments; no disrespectful or improper language; complete in itself; (3) the order-maker
  forwards the appeal with comments and relevant records without avoidable delay and
  without waiting for direction.
- **R27 — Consideration of appeal**: (1) suspension appeals — consider in the light of
  Rule 10 and the circumstances whether justified; confirm or revoke. (2) penalty/
  enhancement appeals — consider (a) whether procedure was complied with and if not,
  whether non-compliance resulted in violation of the Constitution or failure of justice;
  (b) whether findings are warranted by the evidence on record; (c) whether the penalty is
  adequate, inadequate or severe; orders — confirm, enhance, reduce, set aside, or remit
  with directions; provisos — Commission consulted where necessary [2014 2nd Amendment
  added: GS given opportunity of representing against the Commission's advice]; enhanced
  major penalty (v)–(ix) where no Rule 14 inquiry already held → hold/direct inquiry per
  Rule 14 subject to Rule 19; if already held → orders after reasonable opportunity of
  representation; other enhancements → Rule 16-style opportunity. (3) other Rule 23
  orders — consider all circumstances and make just and equitable orders.
- **R28 — Implementation of orders in appeal**: the authority which made the order
  appealed against shall give effect to the orders passed by the appellate authority.
- **R29 — Revision**: (1) notwithstanding anything in these rules — (i) the President;
  (ii) the C&AG (Indian Audit & Accounts Dept); (iii) Member (Personnel) Postal Services
  Board / Adviser (HRD) DoT (Telecommunications Board); (iv) Head of a Department directly
  under the Central Government (not Secretariat/Posts & Telegraphs Board); (v) the
  appellate authority, within six months of the date of the order proposed to be revised;
  (vi) any other authority specified by the President by general/special order, within
  such time as prescribed — may at any time, on own motion or otherwise, call for the
  records and revise any order from which an appeal is allowed but not preferred, or from
  which no appeal is allowed, after UPSC consultation where necessary; may (a) confirm,
  modify or set aside the order; (b) confirm, reduce, enhance or set aside the penalty, or
  impose a penalty; (c) remit the case for further enquiry; (d) pass other orders.
  Proviso 1: no imposing/enhancing a penalty without reasonable opportunity to represent;
  major penalties (v)–(ix) with no prior Rule 14 inquiry → inquiry per Rule 14 subject to
  Rule 19 [+2014: opportunity to represent against the Commission's advice]. Proviso 2:
  C&AG / Member(P) PSB / Adviser(HRD) DoT / HoD may revise only where the authority which
  made the order in appeal, or the authority to which an appeal would lie, is subordinate
  to them. (2) revision proceedings commence only after (i) expiry of the appeal
  limitation period, or (ii) disposal of the appeal. (3) a revision application is dealt
  with as if it were an appeal.
- **R29-A — Review**: the President may at any time, on own motion or otherwise, review
  any order passed under these rules when any new material or evidence which could not be
  produced or was not available at the time of passing the order and which has the effect
  of changing the nature of the case has come, or has been brought, to his notice.
  Proviso: no imposing/enhancing penalty without reasonable opportunity to represent;
  major penalty (or enhancing a minor penalty to major) with no prior Rule 14 inquiry →
  inquiry per Rule 14 subject to Rule 19 and UPSC consultation [+2014: opportunity to
  represent against the Commission's advice].
