# CCA REGEN BATCH R4 — Rule 14 remainder + Rules 17–18 (9 comics + R3.5 regen)

## Verified anchors (fetched this turn: Rule 14 sub-rules 11–23, Rule 17, Rule 18)
- **R14(11)** — notice for discovery/production of Government documents not in the list;
  relevance to be indicated; (12) inquiring authority forwards requisition to custodian,
  may refuse irrelevant documents for recorded reasons; (13) custodian produces; may
  refuse (reasons in writing) where production against public interest or security of
  the State → inform IA → IA informs GS and withdraws requisition.
- **R14(14)** — on the date fixed: oral and documentary evidence produced for the
  disciplinary authority; witnesses examined by PO, cross-examined by/for GS; re-examined
  only on cross-examination points (no new matter without leave); IA may put questions.
- **R14(15)** — before close of DA's case: IA may allow further evidence not listed / call
  new evidence / recall witnesses; GS entitled to copy of the further list + adjournment
  of **3 clear days** (exclusive of adjournment day and next date); inspection before
  record; GS's new evidence if justice requires. NOTE — no gap-filling: only inherent
  lacuna/defect justifies new evidence or recall.
- **R14(16)** — on closure of DA's case: GS states defence orally (recorded + signed) or
  in writing; copy to PO. **(17)** — GS's evidence; may examine himself; his witnesses
  examined, cross-examined, re-examined; IA questions. **(18)** — IA may (and shall if GS
  did not testify) generally question him on circumstances appearing against him.
  **(19)** — IA may hear PO and GS or permit written briefs.
- **R14(20)** — no defence statement / no appearance / failure or refusal to comply →
  inquiry may be held **ex parte**.
- **R14(21)** — minor-penalty DA that has inquired, opinion that (v)–(ix) warranted →
  forward records to the competent DA; that DA may act on the record or recall witnesses
  (examine, cross, re-examine). **(22)** — successor inquiring authority may act on
  predecessor's recorded evidence; may recall witnesses in the interests of justice.
- **R14(23)(i)** — the inquiry report: (a) charges + imputations; (b) defence on each
  article; (c) assessment of evidence per article; (d) findings + reasons. Explanation —
  a different article established may be recorded as a finding only if admitted or
  reasonable opportunity to defend given. **(ii)** — records forwarded to DA: report,
  defence statement, evidence, briefs, orders.
- **R17 — Communication of orders** — orders of the DA communicated to the GS with a copy
  of the findings on each article of charge; where DA is not the IA — its findings with
  brief reasons for disagreement with the IA; where the Commission's advice not accepted
  — brief statement of reasons [words deleted by the 31.10.2014 Second Amendment Rules —
  present text as shown].
- **R18 — Common proceedings** — two or more GSs concerned in a case: the President or
  authority competent to impose dismissal on ALL may order a common proceeding; NOTE —
  different dismissal-level authorities → the highest, with the others' consent;
  (2) order specifies (i) the DA for the proceeding, (ii) the Rule-11 penalties it may
  impose, (iii) the procedure (Rule 14+15 or Rule 16).
