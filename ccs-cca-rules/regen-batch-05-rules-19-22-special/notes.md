# CCA REGEN BATCH R5 — Rules 19–22 (10 comics)

## Verified anchors (fetched this turn: Rules 19, 20, 21, 22)
- **R19 — Special procedure in certain cases** — notwithstanding Rules 14–18, the DA may
  consider the circumstances and make such orders as it deems fit where:
  (i) penalty on the ground of conduct which has led to his conviction on a criminal
  charge (proviso: GS may be given an opportunity of making representation on the
  penalty proposed before any order); (ii) DA satisfied for reasons recorded in writing
  that it is not reasonably practicable to hold an inquiry in the manner provided;
  (iii) President satisfied that in the interest of the security of the State it is not
  expedient to hold any inquiry. Second proviso — Commission consulted where necessary
  [and the GS has been given an opportunity of representing against the advice of the
  Commission — words ADDED by the 31.10.2014 Second Amendment Rules] before orders.
- **R20 — Officers lent to State Governments etc.** — (1) borrowing authority (department/
  State Government/subordinate authority/local or other authority) has the powers of the
  appointing authority for suspension and of the DA for proceedings; proviso — forthwith
  inform the lending authority. (2) findings: (i) minor penalties (i)–(iv) → after
  consultation with the lending authority (difference of opinion → services replaced at
  the lending authority's disposal); (ii) major penalties (v)–(ix) → replace services +
  transmit the inquiry proceedings; lending authority (if DA) passes orders, else submits
  to the DA; proviso — before passing any such order, comply with Rule 15(3) & (4);
  Explanation — may act on the transmitted record or hold further inquiry per Rule 14.
- **R21 — Officers borrowed from State Governments etc.** — mirror provisions where a
  State-borrowed GS serves under the Centre: lending authority informed forthwith;
  minor penalties (i)–(iv) after consultation (difference → replacement), EXCEPT a GS
  serving in the Intelligence Bureau up to the rank of Assistant Central Intelligence
  Officer; major penalties (v)–(ix) → replace + transmit for such action as the lender
  deems necessary; subject to Rule 15(3).
- **R22 — Orders against which no appeal lies** — (i) any order made by the President;
  (ii) any order of an interlocutory nature or of the nature of a step-in-aid of the
  final disposal of a disciplinary proceeding, OTHER than an order of suspension;
  (iii) any order passed by an inquiring authority in the course of an inquiry under
  Rule 14.
